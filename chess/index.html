How are moves calculated?
  1. Legal moves: the chess rules (piece movement, check, castling, en passant, promotion) decide which moves are allowed. Each allowed move counts as 1.
  2. Probability: a move's chance = 1 ÷ number of legal moves. A piece's chance = its moves ÷ all moves. Smart mode adds extra weight to captures, center pawns, development, castling, checks and promotions.
  3. Best move: the engine tries every move, then every reply, then every reply to that (3 half-moves), and assumes both sides choose their best option. Each end position is scored: pawn 1, knight 3, bishop 3, rook 5, queen 9, plus small bonuses for center control, advanced pawns and developed pieces. Checkmate scores highest.
  4. Limits: this is a simple engine. It can miss deeper tactics and is much weaker than programs like Stockfish, so use it as a guide, not a final answer.
